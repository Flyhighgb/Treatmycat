These are the product types our guides recommend most, gathered in one place. We describe types and the features that matter rather than pushing single brands, so you can choose what suits your cat and budget. Each section links to the full guide if you want the detail.

If your cat is unwell, start with your vet, not a product. Nothing here treats illness.

## Scratching posts and cat trees

Scratching is normal, and the right post saves your furniture. Choose one that's tall, sturdy and covered in sisal. Full guide: [how to choose a scratching post](/guides/how-to-choose-a-scratching-post/).

| Type | Best for | Shop |
|---|---|---|
| Tall sisal post | Most cats. Choose 30 inches or taller with a heavy base. | [Tall sisal posts](amazon:tall sisal cat scratching post 32 inch) |
| Cat tree | Climbers, indoor cats and multi-cat homes | [Cat trees](amazon:cat tree sisal scratching posts) |
| Cardboard scratcher | Cats that like to scratch flat, and older cats | [Cardboard scratchers](amazon:cardboard cat scratcher) |
| Wall-mounted scratcher | Small homes. Saves floor space and can't tip over. | [Wall scratchers](amazon:wall mounted cat scratcher) |
