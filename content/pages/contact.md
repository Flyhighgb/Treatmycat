---
title: Contact us
description: How to get in touch with Treat My Cat about a guide, a correction or anything else.
---
We'd love to hear from you. Email us at [hello@treatmycat.com](mailto:hello@treatmycat.com) and Paul will reply, usually within a few days.

**Spotted a mistake?** Tell us which guide and what's wrong. We check every correction against veterinary sources and update the guide, along with its "last reviewed" date.

**Have a question about your own cat?** We're not vets, so we can't diagnose or advise on a particular cat. Please call your vet. In an emergency, contact your vet or the nearest emergency clinic. For a suspected poisoning, you can call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435.

**Brands and advertisers.** We don't accept payment for reviews or for placement in our guides. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for how the site earns money.
