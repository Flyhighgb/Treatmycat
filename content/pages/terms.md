## Medical disclaimer

Treat My Cat publishes general information about cat health and care. It is not veterinary advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan, and it doesn't replace an exam by a licensed veterinarian who knows your cat.

Our guides are written from published veterinary sources, which are listed at the bottom of each guide, but we are not vets. Cats can hide illness, and the same signs can have very different causes. If your cat is unwell, or you're unsure, contact your vet. Never delay or ignore veterinary advice because of something you read here.

**In an emergency**, contact your vet or the nearest emergency clinic right away. For a suspected poisoning, you can also call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435.

We never give medicine doses. Always follow the product label or your vet's instructions, and never give your cat human medicines unless your vet tells you to.
