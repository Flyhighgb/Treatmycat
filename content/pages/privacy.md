title: Privacy policy
description: What data Treat My Cat collects and why.
---
**Last updated: October 2026**

Treat My Cat does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or other personal details.

**Analytics.** We use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page views. It doesn't track you across sites or build a profile of you.

**Affiliate links.** When you click a link to a retailer, that retailer may set its own cookies so it can credit us for a sale. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

**Advertising.** If we show ads in future, ad partners may use cookies to show relevant ads. We will update this page and ask for consent where the law requires it.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
