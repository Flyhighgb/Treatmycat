**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

Treat My Cat does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or other personal details.

**Analytics.** We use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page views. It doesn't track you across sites or build a profile of you.

**Affiliate links.** When you click a link to a retailer, that retailer may set its own cookies so it can credit us for a sale. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

**Advertising.** We show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, measure how they perform, and, where allowed, show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), or opt out of some third-party vendors' personalized ads at [aboutads.info](https://optout.aboutads.info/). Visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
