---
title: About Treat My Cat
description: Who writes Treat My Cat and how the guides are put together.
---
Treat My Cat is a library of practical guides for the everyday problems cat owners run into: fleas, hairballs, upset stomachs, itchy skin, picky eating and more.

Every guide follows the same shape:

- **What's likely going on**, in plain language.
- **What you can safely do at home**, and what you should never do.
- **When it's time to see a vet**, with the warning signs spelled out.

Guides are based on published veterinary guidance from sources such as the American Association of Feline Practitioners, International Cat Care, the RSPCA, Cornell Feline Health Center and the Merck Veterinary Manual. They are general information, not a diagnosis. Your vet knows your cat, so when in doubt, call them.

Spotted something wrong or out of date? We review and update guides regularly.
